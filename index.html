Frequently Asked Questions

Is SkillSwap free?

Yes! Everyone starts with 25 free credits. You earn more by teaching others and receiving good reviews. No money ever changes hands.

Who can join?

Anyone with a skill to share or a desire to learn. Whether you are a professional, student, hobbyist, or retiree — there is a place for you here.

How do sessions work?

Find someone with the skill you want, send them a message, and book a time. Sessions can be in-person or virtual — you decide together.

Is it safe?

All members have public profiles with reviews. Start with short sessions, meet in public places, and trust your instincts. Our review system keeps everyone accountable.
